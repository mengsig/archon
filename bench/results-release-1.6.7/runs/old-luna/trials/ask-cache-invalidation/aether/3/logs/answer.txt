The stale category price comes from **`updatePrice` invalidating only the product cache**. It deletes `product:<sku>`, so the next product-page request reloads the updated price. Category pages use separate `listing:<category>:<page>` cache entries, which `updatePrice` never clears.

A listing stores each product’s `priceCents` in its cached rows. Those rows expire after **600 seconds** (`LISTING_TTL_SECONDS` in [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-a7e9g5pr/archon-bench/work/20261128T072017Z/8b17a6edcb93fd8a31c074f7/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:5)). That leaves the old price visible until the affected listing entry expires: up to ten minutes after the price change, depending on when the entry was created.

The fix is to invalidate every cached listing page for the product’s category when its price changes, as well as the product key. The cache currently only supports deleting a known key, so the service needs a way to track or enumerate category listing keys, or to invalidate them via a category version. The existing tests check that product pages update immediately, but don’t cover a listing after a price change.
