**Critical — authentication bypass** in [`src/tokens.js`, `verify`]( /home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/6a80d2f624da96a23a66e680/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/review-seeded-bug/src/tokens.js:24). The verifier accepts any signature that is a prefix of the expected HMAC. An empty signature is a prefix of every string, so an attacker can submit `<base64url({"user":"admin","exp":9999999999999})>.` and `verify` returns the forged claims. Require an exact-length signature match and compare the full MAC.
