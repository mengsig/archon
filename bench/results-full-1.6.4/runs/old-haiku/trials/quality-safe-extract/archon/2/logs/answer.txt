- **API:** `extract(entries, root) -> list[str]`, returning names in input order.

- **Collisions:** rejects duplicates and file/directory collisions within the batch, in either order.

- **Writes:** missing parents are created and exact bytes are written, including empty files. I/O errors propagate.
- **Tests:** the brief asks for focused tests covering each rule, plus the boundary cases.
