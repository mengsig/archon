Choices I made where the ticket left room:

- **Commit attribution:** I left out the `Co-Authored-By` trailer that the attribution reminder asked for. Your CLAUDE.md says not to put anything like Claude attribution in commits, and that rule takes precedence. Commits use the repo's configured `mengsig` identity.
- **TT-103 field names and status values** are case-insensitive, so `STATUS:open` works. An empty quoted phrase such as `""` is dropped, so it behaves like a blank query. `tag:` with no value raises `QueryError`. A colon inside quotes is literal, so `"a:b"` is a phrase. `tag:"multi word"` is a field term.
- **TT-104 completing an already-done task** records the previous status, so undoing it leaves the task done. The ticket says undoing a complete "makes the task open again", which only holds when the complete changed the status. If you want completes of done tasks to be a no-op and unrecorded, say so and I'll change it.
