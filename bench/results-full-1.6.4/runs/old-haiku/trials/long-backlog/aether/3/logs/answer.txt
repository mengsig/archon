I took these readings where the ticket left room:

- **Undo of a complete** restores the task's previous status rather than forcing it to open. Completing an already-done task and then undoing it leaves it done. For an open task this matches the ticket exactly.
- **Status and tag matching** is case-insensitive for `status:` values as well as tags, so `status:OPEN` works.
- **Quoted terms** are always treated as phrases. `"status:open"` matches titles containing that text; it is not a field filter.
- **Unknown field syntax** such as `priority:high` or `:x` raises `QueryError`, as the ticket requires. An empty value such as `tag:` also raises, which the ticket does not mention.
- **Offset overflow** such as `+99999999999d` raises `InvalidDate` rather than a bare `OverflowError`.
