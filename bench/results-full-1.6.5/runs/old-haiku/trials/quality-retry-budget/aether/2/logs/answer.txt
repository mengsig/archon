I implemented `retry_delay` in `retry_budget.py` per `SPEC.md`, added 26 focused tests, and committed the work as `3e1039d` on `main`. All 26 tests pass with `python3 -m unittest -v test_retry_budget`.

- **Validation:** every argument is checked before any early return. Values must be built-in ints (not bool or float) and meet the minimums in the spec, otherwise `ValueError`.
- **Attempt limit:** `attempt >= max_attempts` returns `None`.
- **Backoff:** `min(cap_ms, base_ms * 2**(attempt-1))`. A zero base or zero cap gives 0. Large attempt values return `cap_ms` directly, without building the power of two.
- **Retry-After:** used as an uncapped floor via `max(backoff, retry_after_ms)`.
- **Budget:** returns `None` when `elapsed_ms + delay >= budget_ms`. The delay is never shortened to fit.
