The root cause is the listing cache invalidation gap in [`src/pricing.js`](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-p9b3h69d/archon-bench/work/20261120T084439Z/f1ad05d97cca992d99371374/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js) and [`src/catalog.js`](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-p9b3h69d/archon-bench/work/20261120T084439Z/f1ad05d97cca992d99371374/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js).

`updatePrice` writes the new price to the store and deletes the cached `product:<sku>` entry. That makes the product page reload the updated product immediately. Category pages use separate `listing:<category>:<page>` entries. The listing method caches rows—including `priceCents`—but the price update never deletes or refreshes those entries, so they keep returning the old price until their TTL expires.

That delay is up to 600 seconds (10 minutes), as set by `LISTING_TTL_SECONDS` in [`src/config.js`](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-p9b3h69d/archon-bench/work/20261120T084439Z/f1ad05d97cca992d99371374/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js). It is up to ten minutes from when that listing cache entry was created, so a price change can appear to take anywhere from almost no time to ten minutes.

The fix is to invalidate every cached category listing page containing the changed SKU after updating its price, or use a versioned/category-aware cache strategy that makes affected listing entries miss immediately. Deleting only the product cache entry cannot refresh cached listing snapshots. No code was changed.
