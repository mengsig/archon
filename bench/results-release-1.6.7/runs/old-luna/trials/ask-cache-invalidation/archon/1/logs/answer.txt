The category listing cache is responsible. `Catalog.listing()` caches each category and page under `listing:<category>:<page>` for `LISTING_TTL_SECONDS` ([catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-a7e9g5pr/archon-bench/work/20261128T072017Z/65655740bc527856a045e2e2/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22), [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-a7e9g5pr/archon-bench/work/20261128T072017Z/65655740bc527856a045e2e2/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:7)). That TTL is 600 seconds. A listing already in cache can therefore keep its old price until its remaining TTL expires—up to ten minutes after the merchant’s change.

`updatePrice()` updates the store and deletes only the product’s cache entry ([pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-a7e9g5pr/archon-bench/work/20261128T072017Z/65655740bc527856a045e2e2/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:10)). That’s why the product page refreshes immediately while category listings remain stale.

The fix is to invalidate the affected category’s cached listing pages when its product price changes. The current cache has no category-level invalidation mechanism, so add one—such as tracking listing keys by category or supporting category-prefix invalidation—and call it from `updatePrice()`. This should cover every cached page for that category, not just page zero.
