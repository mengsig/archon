The stale category prices come from the cached listing path in [catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/a262c4edad72eb3be503f3ae/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22). `listing()` caches a snapshot of each category page, including `priceCents`, under a category-and-page key.

When a merchant changes a price, [pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/a262c4edad72eb3be503f3ae/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:8) updates the store and deletes only that SKU’s product-page cache entry. It doesn’t invalidate any listing cache containing that SKU. Product pages therefore reload immediately, while category pages keep serving their cached snapshots until they expire.

The delay is the listing cache TTL: [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/a262c4edad72eb3be503f3ae/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js) sets it to about ten minutes. The fix is to invalidate the affected category listing page keys when a price changes, alongside the product key. If listings may span multiple pages, invalidate every cached page for that category, or use a category version/generation in listing keys so one increment makes all old pages unreachable.
