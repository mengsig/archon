The stale category price comes from `Catalog.listing()` in [src/catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-kct3x9mo/archon-bench/work/20261120T084524Z/92c7837c2efbb525ca39f36a/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22). It caches the assembled category page under `listing:<category>:<page>` and returns that cached snapshot until it expires.

When a merchant changes a price, `updatePrice()` updates the store and deletes only the product’s cache entry ([src/pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-kct3x9mo/archon-bench/work/20261120T084524Z/92c7837c2efbb525ca39f36a/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:6)). That’s why the product page sees the new price immediately while category pages can remain stale.

The delay is up to 600 seconds: [src/config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-kct3x9mo/archon-bench/work/20261120T084524Z/92c7837c2efbb525ca39f36a/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:1) sets `LISTING_TTL_SECONDS` to 600, and [src/cache.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-kct3x9mo/archon-bench/work/20261120T084524Z/92c7837c2efbb525ca39f36a/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/cache.js:20) expires an entry 600 seconds after it was populated. The observed wait depends on how long ago that listing was cached.

The fix is to invalidate the affected category’s listing cache entries when `updatePrice()` runs, including every page for that category. The current listing keys include category and page but there’s no category-wide invalidation mechanism. A key index/tag per category, or a cache-generation/version in listing keys, would let the price update invalidate those pages immediately.
