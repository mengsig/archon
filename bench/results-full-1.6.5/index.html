Reading these results

Intervals are 95% Wilson for pass rates and percentile bootstrap for ratios of means. McNemar is an exact paired test; wall and cost p-values use Mann–Whitney. Three trials per task leave substantial uncertainty. Cost includes billed and estimated usage.

Independent e2e validation overlapped the Haiku runs by 08:32 UTC. Haiku wall-time and timeout comparisons are confounded by this workload; timeout-related pass/fail outcomes may also be affected.

See README.md and summary.json in this directory for provenance, run data, exclusions, flipped tasks, and limitations.
