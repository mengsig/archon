Assumptions I made where the ticket was silent or left room:
- **TT-104, undo of complete:** the log records the task's previous status and restores it, rather than forcing `open`. These differ only when a task was already `done` before `complete` was called on it.
- **TT-103, quotes:** a term that starts with a quote is a literal phrase, so `"tag:work"` is a title search. Quotes inside a field value only group whitespace, so `tag:"big project"` matches the tag `big project`.
- **TT-103, empty or odd values:** `tag:` with no name raises `QueryError`. `status:` values are case-insensitive, matching tags. Any `x:y` term with an unknown field raises, including something like `10:30`.
- **Commit messages:** I left out the Co-Authored-By line from the reminder. CLAUDE.md says not to add Claude attribution, and it takes precedence.
