The stale category price comes from the listing cache. `Catalog.listing()` copies each product’s `priceCents` into a cached page under a category-and-page key ([catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-a7e9g5pr/archon-bench/work/20261128T072017Z/16db243a9d4e725dbd8dccd9/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22)). `updatePrice()` updates the store and evicts only that SKU’s product cache entry ([pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-a7e9g5pr/archon-bench/work/20261128T072017Z/16db243a9d4e725dbd8dccd9/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:10)), so product pages fetch the new price while already-cached category pages keep their snapshots.

The delay is the listing TTL: 600 seconds, or ten minutes ([config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-a7e9g5pr/archon-bench/work/20261128T072017Z/16db243a9d4e725dbd8dccd9/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:7)). Once the entry expires, the next listing request rebuilds it from the updated store.

The fix is to invalidate every cached listing page for the product’s category when its price changes, in addition to evicting the product key. The cache needs a way to find those pages, such as a category-to-listing-key index or category version keys. Shortening the TTL would only reduce the stale window, not prevent it.
