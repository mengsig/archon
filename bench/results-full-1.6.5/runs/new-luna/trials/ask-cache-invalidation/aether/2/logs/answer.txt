Category pages use a separate cache path in `Catalog.listing()`: it caches the assembled category page under `listing:<category>:<page>` for `LISTING_TTL_SECONDS`, which is 600 seconds. The cached rows include `priceCents`, so they keep showing the old price until that entry expires.

`updatePrice()` updates the store and deletes only the product cache entry (`product:<sku>`). That makes product pages refresh immediately, but leaves every cached category listing containing that SKU untouched. The roughly ten minute delay is the listing cache’s 600 second TTL.

The fix is to invalidate affected listing entries when a price changes—for example, delete all cached pages for the product’s category after the update. If the cache cannot enumerate keys or map products to categories, use a category-level version key or another targeted invalidation mechanism.
