- **Implementation:** `TokenBucket` and `KeyedLimiter` use only the standard library. The clock is injected, and the spec's `ValueError` cases are all enforced.
- **Tests:** I added 12 tests to `test_ratelimit.py` covering the spec's edge cases: parameter validation, fractional refill, the capacity cap, `wait_time`, clock regression, LRU eviction, and evicted keys returning full.

Choices where the spec was silent:
- `KeyedLimiter` validates `n` before touching the key map, so an invalid call cannot evict another key.
