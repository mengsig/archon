What changed: tasktracker/store.py now keeps an undo log of successful add, complete and delete calls. undo() pops the latest entry and reverts it: an add removes the task, a complete restores the status it had before, and a delete restores a deep-copied snapshot under the same id. Ids are never reused. With an empty log, undo() raises NothingToUndo, defined in tasktracker.store. Failed calls (unknown id, blank title) raise before anything is logged. Decision for the spec owner: BACKLOG.md says undoing a complete "makes the task open again". I implemented "reverts", so completing an already-done task and then undoing it leaves the task done.
