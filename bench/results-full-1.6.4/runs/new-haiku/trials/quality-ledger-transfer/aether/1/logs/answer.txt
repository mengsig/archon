**What changed**
- `ledger.py`: `Ledger(balances)`, `transfer(...)` and `snapshot()`, using only the standard library.
  - Validation rejects bools and non-built-in ints, and requires non-empty string names.
  - Construction and snapshots copy their data, so nothing aliases internal state.
  - Amount is validated before any account lookup. Unknown or non-string accounts raise `KeyError`.
  - A single `threading.Lock` covers every operation. This makes transfers linearizable, keeps snapshots from seeing a half-applied transfer, and means opposite transfers cannot deadlock.
  - Same-account transfers return whether funds suffice and never change the balance, even at `MAX_BALANCE`.
- `test_ledger.py`: kept the two original tests and added focused classes for construction, amount and account validation, boundary and overflow outcomes, self-transfers, and threaded behaviour (opposite transfers, snapshot consistency, and an exact overflow stop at `MAX_BALANCE`).
