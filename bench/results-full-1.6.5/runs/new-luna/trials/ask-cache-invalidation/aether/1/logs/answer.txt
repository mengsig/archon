The stale category prices come from the **listing cache**. `Catalog.listing()` caches each category page under `listing:<category>:<page>` for `LISTING_TTL_SECONDS` ([catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-dwsb1bl5/archon-bench/work/20261125T011222Z/550de21cb81509fbb160796f/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22), [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-dwsb1bl5/archon-bench/work/20261125T011222Z/550de21cb81509fbb160796f/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:7)). That TTL is **600 seconds—10 minutes**. A cached page keeps the old `priceCents` until it expires.

The product page updates immediately because `updatePrice()` deletes the product’s `product:<sku>` cache entry after updating the store ([pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-dwsb1bl5/archon-bench/work/20261125T011222Z/550de21cb81509fbb160796f/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:10)). It does not invalidate any listing entries, whose keys are category and page based ([keys.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-dwsb1bl5/archon-bench/work/20261125T011222Z/550de21cb81509fbb160796f/ask-cache-invalidation/src/keys.js:4)).

The fix is to invalidate the affected category’s listing cache pages when a price changes, alongside the product key. Since category listing keys are paginated, invalidate every page for that category, or add a category-level version/tag mechanism that makes all its cached pages unreachable after an update.
