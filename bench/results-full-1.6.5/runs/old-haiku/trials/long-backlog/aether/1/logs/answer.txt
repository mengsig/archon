Assumptions I made where the tickets were silent or ambiguous:

- **Undo of a complete on an already-done task** sets the task to open, as TT-104 literally says. That can surprise: completing a done task and then undoing it reopens it. Say if you want it to restore the prior status instead.
- **Empty quoted phrases** (`""`) and blank terms are ignored, so they match everything, like a blank query.
- **Field names** (`tag`, `status`) are case-insensitive.
- **`tag:` with no name** raises `QueryError`.
- **Offsets too large to represent** (e.g. `+999999999999d`) raise `InvalidDate` rather than `OverflowError`.
- **Commit trailers:** I did not add the `Co-Authored-By` line that the system reminder asked for, because the project's CLAUDE.md says not to put Claude attribution in commits. Commits use the configured `mengsig` identity.

`BACKLOG.md` and `README.md` are unchanged. The `__pycache__/` directories from the test runs are untracked and not committed.
