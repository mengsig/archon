The stale category prices come from the category listing cache. [catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/b3f1652febc0dba7e6d1322b/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22) caches each category/page result under a `listing:` key, including the `priceCents` values. [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/b3f1652febc0dba7e6d1322b/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:6) sets that cache’s TTL to 600 seconds, so a warmed listing can remain stale for up to ten minutes.

The price update path explains why product pages are current: [pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/b3f1652febc0dba7e6d1322b/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:6) updates the store and deletes the product’s `product:` cache entry, but never invalidates any `listing:` entries containing that product.

The direct fix is to invalidate the affected category’s cached pages when a price changes, alongside the product-key deletion. If listings are shared across instances or category/page keys are hard to enumerate, use a versioned category cache key or a short-lived invalidation mechanism. Lowering the listing TTL would only reduce the stale window, not eliminate the invalidation gap. No code was changed.
