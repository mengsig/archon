I implemented `retry_delay` in `retry_budget.py` per `SPEC.md`, added 26 focused tests, and committed the work as `5612240` on `main`. All tests pass with `python3 -m unittest test_retry_budget`.

- **Validation:** every argument is checked first, before any early return. Only built-in ints are accepted (bool and float are rejected), and the minimums from the spec are enforced. Invalid values raise `ValueError`.
