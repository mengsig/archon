The stale category prices come from the listing cache path in [catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/0dbe8a1285256e39b9530081/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js). `listing()` caches a snapshot of each page’s rows under `listing:<category>:<page>`. In [pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/0dbe8a1285256e39b9530081/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js), `updatePrice()` updates the store and evicts only `product:<sku>`. The listing entries remain cached with the old price.

They stay stale for up to 600 seconds: [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/0dbe8a1285256e39b9530081/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js) sets `LISTING_TTL_SECONDS` to 600, and the TTL cache removes an entry only when it expires on a later read. That explains the roughly ten-minute delay. Product pages update immediately because their product cache key is explicitly deleted during the price update.

The fix is to invalidate affected category listing pages whenever a product price changes. Ideally, track which listing keys exist for that category and evict them; alternatively, use a category-level version in listing keys so a price update makes old entries unreachable. Simply shortening the TTL would reduce the delay but leave the invalidation bug in place.
