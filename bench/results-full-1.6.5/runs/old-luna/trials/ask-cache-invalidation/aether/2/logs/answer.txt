The stale category prices come from the cached listing path in [catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/91543c8cd2a206ca679e7c2e/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:25). On a cache miss, `listing()` reads products from the store, copies each product’s `priceCents` into listing rows, and caches those rows under a category-and-page key.

`updatePrice()` updates the store and deletes only the product cache entry ([pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/91543c8cd2a206ca679e7c2e/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:7)). That makes the product path reload the new price immediately, while existing category listing entries remain unchanged until they expire. Their TTL is 600 seconds—ten minutes ([config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/91543c8cd2a206ca679e7c2e/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:7)—so the delay can be up to about ten minutes from when a listing was cached.

The fix is to invalidate affected listing entries when a price changes. Since listing keys are per category and page, `updatePrice()` could look up the product’s category and delete every cached page for that category. A broader alternative is to version or invalidate the category listing cache whenever any product in that category changes. No code was changed.
