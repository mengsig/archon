**Decisions beyond the spec's literal text**
- **NaN and infinity rejected:** `capacity` and `refill_per_sec` must be positive and finite, and `n` must satisfy `0 < n <= capacity`. The spec only says `<= 0` raises. NaN would slip past a plain `<= 0` check and corrupt state silently.
- **Validation before eviction:** `KeyedLimiter` validates `n` before touching its key table. A bad call therefore can't evict a live key.
- **`wait_time` ulp nudge:** `deficit / rate` can round down so that refilling for that long falls just short of `n`. The result is nudged up with `math.nextafter` until it covers the deficit, so a caller who sleeps `wait_time` and retries succeeds.
- **`max_keys` not type-checked:** only `max_keys >= 1` is enforced, as the spec says. A non-integer such as `1.5` is accepted without complaint.
