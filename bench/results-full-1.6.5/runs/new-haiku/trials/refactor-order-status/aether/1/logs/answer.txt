The status refactor is done and committed as `c5ce65c` on `main`. `cargo test` passes: the 3 existing tests, plus 2 new ones I added for the transition table and parsing.

**What changed**
- **`src/status.rs`** (new): `OrderStatus` with `as_str`, `Display`, `can_transition_to`, and `FromStr`. `ParseStatusError` implements `std::error::Error`, and its message is `unknown status "<input>"`.
- **`Order::status`** is now `OrderStatus`. `pay`, `ship`, `cancel`, `is_open`, `outstanding_cents`, `ready_to_ship` and `from_line` use the enum and its transition rules.
- **`lib.rs`** re-exports `OrderStatus` and `ParseStatusError` from the crate root.
- **`tests/orders.rs`** compares against enum variants. The remaining string literals are wire-format and error-text expectations.

**Behaviour preserved**
- Error messages are unchanged: `from_line` still reports `unknown status "lost"` through the `ParseStatusError` message.
- `status_counts` still returns `Vec<(String, usize)>` sorted by name. I key the map by `as_str()` because sorting by the enum's derived order would reorder the output.
- Parsing is still exact and case-sensitive.
