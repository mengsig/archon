Quota pace: codex's primary window is 68% used with 46% of it elapsed. At this rate it runs out around 2026-11-26 13:19 UTC, before it resets at 2026-11-28 20:29 UTC. Archon throttles nothing; the orchestrator chooses what runs on codex until then.

The stale category price comes from `Catalog.listing()` in [catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-dwsb1bl5/archon-bench/work/20261125T011222Z/c7592c58689b2fe5f645d159/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22). It caches each category/page’s rows under a `listing:<category>:<page>` key. `updatePrice()` in [pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-dwsb1bl5/archon-bench/work/20261125T011222Z/c7592c58689b2fe5f645d159/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:6) deletes only the product’s `product:<sku>` entry, so product reads refresh immediately while cached listing rows remain unchanged.

The delay is up to 600 seconds: [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-dwsb1bl5/archon-bench/work/20261125T011222Z/c7592c58689b2fe5f645d159/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:5) sets `LISTING_TTL_SECONDS` to 600, and [cache.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-dwsb1bl5/archon-bench/work/20261125T011222Z/c7592c58689b2fe5f645d159/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/cache.js:20) expires entries after that TTL. A listing refreshed just before the price change can therefore show the old price for nearly ten minutes.

The fix is to invalidate every cached listing page for the product’s category when its price changes, as well as the product key. That requires capturing the product’s category and deleting the category’s listing keys, or tracking listing keys by category so invalidation can remove them all. Reducing the TTL would only shorten the stale window; it would not ensure category pages update immediately.
