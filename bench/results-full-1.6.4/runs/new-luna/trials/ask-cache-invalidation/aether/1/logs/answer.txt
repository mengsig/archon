The stale prices come from the category listing cache. `Catalog.listing()` caches each category and page under `listing:<category>:<page>`. When a merchant changes a price, `updatePrice()` updates the store and deletes only `product:<sku>`, so the product page fetches the new price immediately while cached listings remain untouched.

Listings have a 600-second TTL in [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-kct3x9mo/archon-bench/work/20261120T084524Z/76d4cf30ba8273f2ef01c94e/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:5). The cache checks expiry on read, so an affected listing can show the old price for up to ten minutes after it was cached.

The fix is to invalidate the listing cache entries affected by a price update—at minimum, every page for that product’s category—alongside the product key in `updatePrice()`. Alternatively, use a broader category cache invalidation mechanism.
