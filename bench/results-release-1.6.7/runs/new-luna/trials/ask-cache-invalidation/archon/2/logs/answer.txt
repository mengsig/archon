The stale price comes from the **category listing cache**. `listing(category, page)` caches a snapshot of each page’s prices under a `listing:<category>:<page>` key ([catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-lzwaa8jj/archon-bench/work/20261128T072020Z/e4a4824b4567d07dc64caa04/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22), [keys.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-lzwaa8jj/archon-bench/work/20261128T072020Z/e4a4824b4567d07dc64caa04/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/keys.js:4)). Those entries expire after **600 seconds**, or ten minutes ([config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-lzwaa8jj/archon-bench/work/20261128T072020Z/e4a4824b4567d07dc64caa04/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:7)). A listing read during that window returns its cached snapshot until it expires.

`updatePrice` updates the store and deletes only `product:<sku>` ([pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/release-167-R4V9TB/r-lzwaa8jj/archon-bench/work/20261128T072020Z/e4a4824b4567d07dc64caa04/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:10)). That is why product pages immediately fetch the new price, while category listing entries remain untouched.

**Fix:** when a price changes, invalidate every cached listing page for that product’s category as well as its product key. A category-to-listing-key index or cache tag makes that invalidation explicit; the listing will then be rebuilt from the updated store on the next request. No code was changed.
