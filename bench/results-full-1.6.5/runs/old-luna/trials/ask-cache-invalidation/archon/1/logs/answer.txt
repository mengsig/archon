The stale category price comes from a cache invalidation gap:

- `updatePrice` updates the store and deletes only `product:<sku>` in [pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/07e405ea1f741afded29a753/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:6). That is why product pages, which read through that key, show the new price immediately.
- Category pages use separate `listing:<category>:<page>` entries in [catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/07e405ea1f741afded29a753/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22). Those cached rows include `priceCents`, and the price update does not evict them.
- Those entries expire after `LISTING_TTL_SECONDS: 600`—ten minutes—in [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/07e405ea1f741afded29a753/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:7). The delay is therefore the remaining lifetime of the listing cache entry, up to ten minutes.

The fix is to invalidate the affected category listing keys when a price changes. If a product’s category is known, evict its cached pages for that category; if listings can span additional dependencies, invalidate them through a shared catalog or version key. Lowering the TTL would only reduce the maximum stale period, not fix the invalidation gap.
