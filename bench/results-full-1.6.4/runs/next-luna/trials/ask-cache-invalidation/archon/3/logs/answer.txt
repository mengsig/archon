The stale price comes from the category listing cache path. `Catalog.listing()` builds category rows with prices and caches each category/page under `listing:<category>:<page>` for `LISTING_TTL_SECONDS` ([catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22), [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/config.js:1)).

`updatePrice()` updates the store and deletes only that product’s cache entry. Product pages therefore reload the new price immediately, while cached category rows remain unchanged until their listing entry expires. The configured listing TTL is 600 seconds, which explains the reported delay of up to about ten minutes ([pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:6), [keys.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/f164-9rntxe1v/archon-bench/work/20261120T085850Z/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/keys.js:1)).

The fix is to invalidate every cached listing page for the product’s category after a price update, alongside the product key. That requires knowing the category (for example, capture it before updating) and clearing the corresponding `listing:<category>:<page>` entries, or maintaining a category-to-listing-key index. Updating only the TTL would change the maximum delay but would not make listing prices update immediately.
