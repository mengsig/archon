Both repositories are updated and committed: `ledger` at `3002c80` and `reports` at `bd321e8`. `cargo test` passes 8 of 8 and `python3 -m unittest` passes 6 of 6. I also ran the pipeline end to end, and it produces the expected per-account, per-currency totals.

**ledger**
- A TSV line may now carry a fourth column for the currency. A missing column means `USD`.
- The header is `date,account,amount_cents,currency`, and every row carries its currency.
- A malformed currency code (anything other than three uppercase ASCII letters, including an empty fourth column) prints `line N: ...` to stderr and exits with status 2.
- Other malformed input still exits 1. This is the new `ParseError` split in `src/lib.rs`.
- New tests cover currency defaulting and validation, plus a binary-level check of the exit codes and the line number on stderr.

**reports**
- Totals are keyed by `(account, currency)` and sorted by account, then currency. The header is `account,currency,total_cents`.
- CSV without a `currency` column counts as `USD`.
- A malformed currency code prints `reports: line N: ...` to stderr and exits 2.
- New tests cover the per-currency totals, the legacy format, bad and empty currency codes, and the line number in the error.

Both READMEs describe the new input, output, and exit codes.
