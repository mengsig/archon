Choices I made where the ticket was silent:
- **Undo of a complete on an already-done task** restores the previous status, so it stays done.
- **Field names and `status:` values** are case-insensitive, so `TAG:x` and `status:OPEN` work.
- **`tag:` with no value and `status:` with no value** raise `QueryError`.
- **`tag:"my tag"`** is read as the tag `my tag`, since quotes are stripped inside a field value.
- **Overflowing offsets** such as `+999999999d` raise `InvalidDate` rather than `OverflowError`.
- **Year 0000** is rejected as an impossible date.
