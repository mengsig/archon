The stale category price comes from the listing cache path in [catalog.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/e2fae1da1475438f9621bdaf/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/catalog.js:22). `listing()` caches each category and page under its own key, including the product’s `priceCents`. When a merchant changes a price, [pricing.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/e2fae1da1475438f9621bdaf/.archon/data/orchestrator/repos/ask-cache-invalidation/src/pricing.js:6) updates the store and deletes only that SKU’s product cache entry. It leaves all cached category pages intact.

Those listing entries expire after 600 seconds (10 minutes), as configured in [config.js](/home/mengsig/.local/share/archon-bench/full-165-Q4J35ZNK/r-p5gy6w9p/archon-bench/work/20261125T004922Z/e2fae1da1475438f9621bdaf/.archon/data/orchestrator/src/config.js:1). A product page reads through its separate product cache key; deleting that key makes the next product read fetch the updated store value. A category page keeps serving its cached snapshot until it expires, which matches the reported delay.

The fix is to invalidate every cached listing page for the changed product’s category when `updatePrice` runs, alongside deleting the product key. That requires finding or tracking the affected listing keys (including all page numbers). Shortening the listing TTL would reduce the maximum staleness but would not make price changes appear immediately.
