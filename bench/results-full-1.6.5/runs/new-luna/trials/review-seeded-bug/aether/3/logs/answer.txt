- **Critical — `verify` (line 27): signature prefix accepted.** `expected.startsWith(signature)` accepts any prefix, including an empty signature. A token made from a valid body plus a trailing dot (`<body>.`) passes the signature check; if its payload has a future `exp`, it verifies without a MAC.
